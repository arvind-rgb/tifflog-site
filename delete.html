Delete your account.

You can delete your Tiff Log account and everything in it at any time. It can't be undone.

In the app (instant)

Open Tiff Log → Me → danger_zone → DELETE EVERYTHING. Your account, tiffs, people, rules, invite data and encryption key are deleted right away.

Without the app

Email hello@tifflog.ai from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Tiff Log account". We'll confirm it's you and delete everything within 30 days, usually much sooner, and email you when it's done.

Delete some data, keep your account

You don't have to delete your account to remove things. In the app:

  • One tiff: open the tiff → scroll down → delete this tiff. The tiff, its notes and its AI answers are deleted right away.
  • A person: Me → tap the person → edit → remove. Their tiffs and rules are deleted with them.
  • A rule: Me → Your rules → retire.
  • Usage history (which features you used, kept for app analytics): email hello@tifflog.ai from your sign-in email and we'll delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your sign-in email, your encrypted profile and tiffs, the tap-to-pick labels kept for stats, your people and rules, your invite code and Peace Points, your app usage history, and the backup of your encryption key. Removed from our database right away, and from backups within 30 days.

We may keep aggregate counts that can't identify you (like total AI requests per day). Anything you shared outside the app (a copied message, a Wrapped slide) is up to you.
